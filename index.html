Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Kanimambo ("we," "our," or "us") built the xprepare mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the App.

1. Zero Data Collection & Offline Operation

aus_prepare is designed to function completely offline. We do not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, or geolocation data on external servers.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.
  • No Location Tracking: The App does not access, collect, or track your GPS or network-based geolocation.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Cloud SDKs: The App does not use Firebase, advertising networks, crash-reporting trackers, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

2. Local Device Storage

All information you enter into the App—such as emergency preparedness plans, checklists, contacts, and personal notes—is stored exclusively locally on your device. We have no access to the data stored inside your App.

3. User-Initiated Backups and Family Sharing

The App allows you to export and back up your local data or share your emergency preparedness information directly with family members:

  • User-Controlled Transfers: Any backup file export or family sharing action is initiated strictly by you using your device's built-in sharing or file-management tools.
  • Responsibility for Exported Files: Once you export a backup file or share data with family members (for example, via email, messaging apps, or local storage), that shared copy is governed by the privacy and security practices of the recipients and services you choose to use.

4. Data Security

Because all data remains locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers, the security of your information relies on your device's built-in operating system protections (such as screen locks, device encryption, and biometric authentication). We recommend keeping your device operating system up to date and securing exported backup files in a trusted location.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or permanently delete individual entries or clear all stored local data directly within the App at any time.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the App from your device permanently deletes all locally stored App data (excluding any backup files you manually exported to external folders or shared with others).

6. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are added to the App. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date."
